    Inclusion method
    1. The collection has a documented peak floor of 0.1 ETH or more and documented lifetime trading volume of 25 ETH or more. Both are required; either alone is not enough.
    2. Lifetime volume means cumulative collection trading volume, not a rolling window. It is read from the OpenSea V2 collection stats endpoint through a server-side provider. Where no key is configured it is reported as unavailable and the collection's qualification stays pending — a 24h volume is never substituted for it.
    3. The peak-floor condition is historical. A collection that once met it remains a qualifier even if today's floor is lower.
    4. Launch date, mint price, and supply are attributable to the project or to a publication, and are shown at the granularity the source supports — a month stays a month.
    5. Peak floor is stated only where a single figure is sourced. Where it is not, the page shows the sourced high-water event instead of a number.
    6. Live floors come from the CoinGecko NFT collections API and OpenSea, and are never mixed with historical figures without a label.

    ⚠ Floor price is the cheapest listing, not a clearing price for the collection. Survivorship bias is severe: almost no NFT launch reaches these floors. Avoid leverage and predefine position size.
